Verdict: which electric SUV should you buy?

In Malaysia, the BYD Atto 2 (RM100,000) undercuts the Proton e.MAS 7 (from RM105,800) by about RM5,800, making it the cheaper point of entry. The e.MAS 7 hits back with a larger 61 kWh-class battery, up to 410 km of range, 215 hp and a 461-litre boot.

If you want the lowest price and a compact city EV, the Atto 2 is the smarter buy. If you prioritise space, range and performance for family or long-distance use, the e.MAS 7 justifies its small premium.

Key differences at a glance

  • Price: BYD Atto 2 RM100,000; Proton e.MAS 7 from RM105,800.
  • Battery & range: Atto 2 51.1 kWh / 345 km; e.MAS 7 up to 60.22 kWh / 410 km.
  • Power: Atto 2 174 hp / 290 Nm; e.MAS 7 215 hp / 320 Nm.
  • 0–100 km/h: Atto 2 7.9 s; e.MAS 7 6.9 s.
  • Boot space: Atto 2 380 L; e.MAS 7 461 L (+51 L under-floor).
  • Segment: Atto 2 is a B-Segment SUV; e.MAS 7 is a larger C-Segment SUV.

Frequently asked questions

Is the BYD Atto 2 or Proton e.MAS 7 better?

It depends on your priorities. The BYD Atto 2 is the better value pick at RM100,000 and is easier to live with in the city. The Proton e.MAS 7 offers more space, up to 410 km of range and stronger performance, making it the better family EV. Buy the Atto 2 to save money; buy the e.MAS 7 for space and range.

What is the price difference between the BYD Atto 2 and Proton e.MAS 7?

The BYD Atto 2 is priced at RM100,000, while the Proton e.MAS 7 starts from RM105,800 — a difference of about RM5,800 in favour of the Atto 2. The e.MAS 7 Premium tops out around RM123,800.

Which one has a longer range?

The Proton e.MAS 7 has the longer range. Its Prime variant quotes 345 km, matching the Atto 2, while the Premium extends to 410 km on a larger 60.22 kWh battery. The BYD Atto 2 quotes 345 km from its 51.1 kWh pack.

Which is more powerful?

The Proton e.MAS 7 is more powerful, producing 215 hp and 320 Nm against the BYD Atto 2's 174 hp and 290 Nm. The e.MAS 7 also accelerates from 0–100 km/h in 6.9 seconds versus 7.9 seconds.

Which has more boot space?

The Proton e.MAS 7 has a 461-litre boot with an additional 51 litres of under-floor storage. The BYD Atto 2 offers 380 litres, so the e.MAS 7 is the more practical choice for luggage.

Are both cars covered by a battery warranty?

Yes. Both the BYD Atto 2 and Proton e.MAS 7 offer an eight-year / 160,000 km warranty on the battery and EV components, on top of their vehicle warranty.
