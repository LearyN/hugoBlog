Verdict: petrol sedan or compact EV?

These two cars sit on opposite sides of the fuel divide. The Honda City (from RM84,900) undercuts the BYD Atto 2 (RM100,000) by about RM15,100 on purchase price — but the Atto 2 claws that back at the pump: charging costs roughly RM0.08/km against ~RM0.20/km for the City's petrol.

If you drive a lot and can charge at home, the Atto 2 wins on running cost and offers instant torque with zero tailpipe emissions. If your annual mileage is low, you rely on street parking without a charger, or you want the lowest upfront price and proven resale, the City is the safer, simpler buy.

Key differences at a glance

  • Fuel type: Honda City is 1.5L petrol; BYD Atto 2 is a battery-electric (BEV).
  • Price: City from RM84,900; Atto 2 RM100,000.
  • "Refuelling": City fills in ~5 min at any station; Atto 2 charges in ~30 min DC / hours on AC.
  • Energy cost: City ~RM0.20/km (petrol); Atto 2 ~RM0.08/km (home charging).
  • Power: City 121 hp; Atto 2 174 hp / 290 Nm (instant torque).
  • 0–100 km/h: City 10.4 s; Atto 2 7.9 s.
  • Boot space: City 519 L; Atto 2 380 L.

Which one is right for your lifestyle?

  • High-mileage drivers with a home charger: the Atto 2 — the more you drive, the more the EV saves.
  • Low-mileage or apartment dwellers: the City — no charger dependency, lowest entry price.
  • Long-distance / rural drivers: the City — petrol stations everywhere, no charging planning.
  • Performance seekers: the Atto 2 — 174 hp and a 7.9 s sprint beat the City's 121 hp.

Frequently asked questions

Should I buy a petrol Honda City or an electric BYD Atto 2?

It depends on how you drive and where you park. The petrol Honda City is cheaper upfront (RM84,900) and refuels anywhere in five minutes, making it ideal for low-mileage or long-distance drivers. The electric BYD Atto 2 (RM100,000) costs far less to run (roughly RM0.08/km on home charging versus RM0.20/km) and is quicker, so it wins for high-mileage drivers with a home charger.

How much cheaper is electric to run than petrol?

On home charging, the BYD Atto 2 costs roughly RM8 per 100 km, against around RM20.50 per 100 km for the petrol Honda City — about 2.5 times cheaper. DC fast charging is closer to petrol prices, so home charging is what makes the EV pay off.

Which one is more powerful?

The electric BYD Atto 2 is more powerful, producing 174 hp and 290 Nm of instant torque against the Honda City's 121 hp and 145 Nm. The Atto 2 accelerates from 0–100 km/h in 7.9 seconds versus 10.4 seconds for the City.

Is range anxiety a problem with the EV?

The BYD Atto 2 quotes 345 km of range on a full charge, while the petrol Honda City can cover around 740 km per tank and refuel in minutes. For daily commuting the EV is fine, but for frequent long-distance or rural driving the petrol car is more convenient.

Which has more boot space, the sedan or the SUV?

The Honda City sedan has the larger boot at 519 litres, against 380 litres in the BYD Atto 2 SUV. However, the Atto 2 is wider and taller with a higher seating position, so it feels roomier for occupants.

Do both cars come with advanced driver assistance?

The Honda City includes Honda SENSING as standard across the range. The BYD Atto 2 also offers ADAS including autonomous emergency braking, lane-keeping assist and a 360-degree camera. Confirm the exact variant coverage before buying.
