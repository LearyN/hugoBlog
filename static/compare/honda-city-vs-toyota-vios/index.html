Verdict: which B-segment sedan should you buy?

In Malaysia, the Honda City (from RM84,900) undercuts the Toyota Vios (from RM89,600) by about RM4,700, making it the cheaper point of entry. The City also hits harder with a 121 hp 1.5L i-VTEC engine, a 519-litre boot and Honda SENSING driver assistance across the range.

If you want the sharper drive, more standard safety tech and the bigger boot, the City is the smarter buy. If you prioritise resale value, brand reliability and proven running-cost stability, the Vios remains the safe, sensible choice.

Key differences at a glance

  • Price: Honda City from RM84,900; Toyota Vios from RM89,600.
  • Engine: City 1.5L i-VTEC; Vios 1.5L Dual VVT-i.
  • Power: City 121 hp / 145 Nm; Vios 104 hp / 138 Nm.
  • Fuel economy: City ~5.4 L/100km; Vios ~5.5 L/100km.
  • 0–100 km/h: City 10.4 s; Vios 11.5 s (City quicker).
  • Boot space: City 519 L; Vios 475 L.

Safety and driver assistance

The Honda City comes with 6 airbags, ABS/EBD, stability control, and Honda SENSING as standard across the range — including autonomous emergency braking (AEB), lane-keeping assist, adaptive cruise control and lane-departure warning.

The Toyota Vios also lists 6 airbags, ABS/EBD and stability control, with Toyota Safety Sense (AEB, lane-tracing assist, adaptive cruise) available on higher trims. On paper both are well-equipped; confirm the exact ADAS list against the latest variant spec sheet.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Honda City or Toyota Vios better?

It depends on your priorities. The Honda City is the better value pick at RM84,900 and offers more power, larger boot space and standard Honda SENSING. The Toyota Vios counters with best-in-class resale value and lower running costs. Buy the City for performance and kit; buy the Vios for reliability and resale.

What is the price difference between the Honda City and Toyota Vios?

The Honda City starts from RM84,900, while the Toyota Vios starts from RM89,600 — a difference of about RM4,700 in favour of the City. The City tops out around RM112,900 and the Vios around RM103,800.

Which one is more powerful?

The Honda City is more powerful, producing 121 hp and 145 Nm from its 1.5L i-VTEC engine against the Toyota Vios's 104 hp and 138 Nm from its 1.5L Dual VVT-i. The City also accelerates from 0–100 km/h in 10.4 seconds versus 11.5 seconds.

Which has better fuel economy?

Both are closely matched. The Honda City returns around 5.4 L/100km combined, while the Toyota Vios returns around 5.5 L/100km. Both run on RON 95 petrol, so refuelling costs are nearly identical.

Which has more boot space?

The Honda City has the larger boot at 519 litres, against 475 litres in the Toyota Vios. If luggage space matters, the City is the more practical choice.

Do both cars come with advanced driver assistance?

The Honda City includes Honda SENSING as standard across the range. The Toyota Vios offers Toyota Safety Sense on higher trims. Both systems include autonomous emergency braking and adaptive cruise control; confirm the exact variant coverage before buying.
